HOW TO PLAY

Twenty-Nine Rules

Our first version uses the 6-point game variation.

01 · The deck

32 cards: 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K and A in all four suits.

02 · Card ranking

J > 9 > A > 10 > K > Q > 8 > 7.

03 · Card points

Jack = 3, Nine = 2, Ace = 1, Ten = 1. Other cards = 0.

04 · Bidding

After four cards each, bidding starts at 16 and can rise to 28. The highest bidder selects trump.

05 · Tricks

Players must follow suit when possible. A trump card beats a non-trump card. Eight tricks are played.

06 · Winning

The declaring team must meet its bid. The first team to reach 6 game points wins.
