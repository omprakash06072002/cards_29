HOW TO PLAY

Twenty-Nine Rules

01 · Deck

32 cards: 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K and A in all four suits.

02 · Ranking

J > 9 > A > 10 > K > Q > 8 > 7.

03 · Points

J = 3, 9 = 2, A = 1, 10 = 1. K, Q, 8, 7 = 0. Last trick adds 1.

04 · Bidding

Four cards are dealt first. Bidding starts at 16 and rises to 28. Three consecutive passes end bidding. Highest bidder chooses trump.

05 · Tricks

After the second deal each player has 8 cards. Follow the led suit if you can. A trump beats a non-trump suit. Highest valid card wins the trick.

06 · Scoring

The declaring team must meet its bid. If it does, it gains 1 game point; if it fails, it loses 1 game point. The first team to the selected target wins.

07 · Optional 7th-card peek

The bidder may reveal the reserved 7th card before choosing trump. If that suit is selected, the hand is marked as a peek hand; a failed bid carries the double-loss penalty.
